## Fallback vs native guarantees (verified by tests, not equivalent)

The default `fallback` mode performs **no Reddit write**: the player copies the exact body and
publishes it themselves. Tests assert that fallback creates no comment, **no parent thread**, no
index entry, is not counted by the Signal Board, leaves the player able to contribute, and exposes
**no** minority reveal. A manually pasted comment is real and reportable on Reddit but is outside the
app's index, so `onCommentDelete` cannot desindex it (asserted as a no-op).

Persistence, one-note-per-chapter, idempotent retry, board counting, the correct-minority reveal and
delete-desindexing are **native-only** guarantees. The simulation numbers above are native-mode.
