# Sensor design

## Echo

- Gesture: up to three taps.
- Progression: continuity; cavity/depth; fragility/contradiction.
- Language: cyan contours, segmented waves, secondary cavity rings, fractured returns.
- Excludes: temperature, biological heat, airflow, contamination, and result.

## Heat

- Gesture: press and hold a region; early release after a short intentional hold produces a lower-clarity reading.
- Progression: localized gradients; living presence/motion; volatility rhythm.
- Language: localized organic glows, shape-count signatures, ghost marks, concentric volatility rings.
- Excludes: cavity, structure stability, airflow, contamination, and result.

## Wind

- Gesture: short tolerant drag; activation occurs once the drag crosses 18 px and remains capped at three.
- Progression: general flow; eddies/crosscurrents; carried contamination.
- Language: curved tracer paths, turbulence loops, and contamination-specific circles/rectangles/triangles.
- Excludes: cavity, temperature, biological heat, and result.

All senses provide text descriptions, mouse/touch input, a three-use cap, replay/reset, listener cleanup, and reduced-motion rendering. Direction nodes require their own press origin so a sensor gesture cannot accidentally decide.
